Good to know

Questions, answered.

How can I find out about upcoming classes?

Use the enquiry form below to ask the studio about current schedules, formats and availability. This demo does not display unverified class dates.

Are painting sessions suitable for beginners?

Let the studio know your experience level in the enquiry form so they can recommend a suitable option.

Can I enquire about children's art activities?

Yes. Select “Children's art programs” in the form and ask about current age groups and availability.

Can I request a custom painting?

Yes. Choose “Custom artwork / commission” and describe your idea, approximate size and preferred timeline.
